Screen Calibration

Hold a standard credit/ID card flat against the screen. Drag the slider until the box below exactly matches the card's width edge-to-edge. This lets the app calculate this iPad's true pixel density, so every optotype size shown afterward is physically accurate.

Card reference: ISO ID-1 standard, 85.60mm wide (any standard credit, debit, or driver's license card).
